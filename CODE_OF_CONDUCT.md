# CraftCV code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to contribute regardless of background, identity, or experience. Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Credit others' work, respect privacy, and accept feedback in good faith.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content directed at participants, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. These expectations apply to project discussions, issues, reviews, and other project spaces.

Report concerns privately to project maintainers through an available private project contact. Do not include sensitive information in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect privacy where possible, and may warn, remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban participants for violations. Maintainers are also accountable to this policy. Retaliation against someone reporting a concern is prohibited.
